# Sponsored Listings and Ranking Disclosure

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 23 July 2026

## 1. Labels

Listings receiving paid visibility will be clearly labelled using terms such as “Paid”, “Premium”, “Featured” or “Sponsored”. The label indicates purchased advertising treatment only.

## 2. Main ranking factors

Search and display order may consider:

- the user’s search, category, locality and radius;
- relevance of title/description and listing completeness;
- recency, availability and expiry;
- purchased promotional status and remaining promotional period;
- quality, duplication, spam, fraud, trust and safety signals;
- device/display constraints and service experiments; and
- legal or moderation restrictions.

My Classifieds does not publish exact weights, source code or the proprietary internal priority formula.

## 3. No endorsement

Promotion does not mean that My Classifieds has verified or recommends the advertiser/listing. It does not guarantee top position, uninterrupted placement, enquiries, sale, hire or transaction.

## 4. Moderation prevails

Payment never overrides the Listing Rules. A promoted listing can be held, moved, labelled, restricted or removed. Refund consequences are governed by the Refund Policy.

## 5. Fair design

The interface must not:

- disguise a paid listing as an organic result;
- use a Company verification badge for paid placement;
- fabricate demand, scarcity, countdowns or views;
- preselect an add-on or hide the total price; or
- make it harder to choose Free or decline an optional feature.

## 6. Changes

Ranking evolves to improve relevance, safety and performance. A material change to the effect of paid promotion will be reflected prospectively in this disclosure and checkout.
