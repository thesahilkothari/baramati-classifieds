# Accessibility Statement

**Last reviewed:** 23 July 2026

My Classifieds aims to provide reasonable accessibility, privacy and transparency to users, including users with disabilities.

We seek to:

- support keyboard navigation and visible focus;
- use semantic headings, form labels and descriptive links;
- provide sufficient contrast and scalable text;
- provide text alternatives for meaningful images;
- avoid colour-only instructions and unnecessary motion;
- present errors in clear language;
- make legal pages readable in English and Marathi;
- preserve access to grievance and account-deletion mechanisms; and
- test essential mobile flows with assistive technology where reasonably practicable.
