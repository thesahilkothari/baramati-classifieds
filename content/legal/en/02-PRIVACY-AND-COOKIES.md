## 5. Legal grounds and consent

Where consent is required, it must be free, specific, informed and given through a clear affirmative action. Necessary service processing is separated from optional marketing, non-essential analytics, public telephone display and precise location.

You may withdraw consent through account settings, the method shown at collection, or the privacy contact. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing and may prevent a feature that requires the data from continuing.

## 6. Public advertisement data

Advertisement content and the contact information you choose to expose are public and may be viewed, copied or indexed. Use only the locality and contact information necessary for the advertisement. Do not publish an exact residential address unless genuinely necessary and safe.

Where possible, the product should default to platform contact controls rather than publicly displaying the full telephone number. If you choose a WhatsApp link or public telephone display, the consequence must be shown before publication.

## 9. Retention

We retain data only for the purpose, safety, dispute and legal period described below:

| Record | Standard period |
|---|---|
| Active account/profile | while active |
| Registration information after cancellation/withdrawal | at least 180 days |
| Published/expired ad and moderation history | generally 180 days after expiry/removal; longer for dispute/legal hold |
| Removed content and associated records | at least 180 days, or longer if lawfully required |
| OTP/security/application logs | rolling 180 days where part of ICT/security logs |
| Grievance, report, IP and enforcement file | 3 years after closure; content/evidence minimums still apply |
| Payment, invoice, accounting and tax records | up to 8 financial years or longer where law requires |
| Policy acceptance and consent evidence | account duration plus 3 years, or longer for an active dispute |
| Optional marketing data | until withdrawal, plus a minimal suppression record to honour opt-out |
| Backups | overwritten on the documented backup cycle unless isolated by legal/security hold |

When a period ends, data is deleted, anonymised or securely isolated unless a legal hold applies.

## 14. Cookies and similar technologies

### Essential

Essential cookies/local storage support session security, OTP flow, language, consent preference, load balancing and fraud prevention. They cannot be disabled through the consent panel if the service cannot work without them.

### Optional analytics

Analytics cookies operate only if enabled in production and, where required, after consent. They measure aggregate use and performance. Advertising pixels must remain off unless separately approved, documented and consented to.

The cookie panel must offer:

- “Use essential only”;
- “Allow analytics”;
- “Manage preferences”; and
- an equally easy withdrawal method.

Do not use pre-ticked consent, forced action, false urgency or confusing button design.
