**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 23 July 2026

## 3. Accuracy and substantiation

Maintain evidence for price, performance, comparison, discount, scarcity, stock, origin, warranty, testimonial, certification, licence, delivery and result claims. Disclose material terms before a consumer commits.

## 5. Enquiry data

Use enquiry data only to respond and transact as reasonably expected. Obtain separate consent before unrelated marketing. Maintain access security and delete data no longer needed.

## 6. Paid services

Business listing purchases follow the Refund Policy. Charges are for advertising services and do not depend on commercial results. Taxes will be stated on the Company’s invoice as applicable.

To the extent permitted by law, you indemnify the Company and its officers for third-party claims, consumer orders, penalties and reasonable costs arising from your content, goods/services, transaction, licence, tax, privacy conduct or breach, except to the extent caused by the Company.

## 8. Suspension

The Company may request documents, correct classification, restrict ads, refuse promotion, suspend or terminate for risk/non-compliance. Lawful consumer and regulator cooperation is mandatory.

## 9. Business dispute process

First use the Grievance Policy. The parties may voluntarily mediate a business-to-business dispute. Any separate arbitration must be contained in a specifically accepted written commercial agreement; these public terms do not remove statutory consumer or regulator jurisdiction.
